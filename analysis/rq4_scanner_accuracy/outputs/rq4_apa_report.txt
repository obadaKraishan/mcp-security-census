------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Interpretation
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Precision was highest for DEP_unpinned_dependency (0.93) and lowest for CI_command_injection (0.17). Classes defined by a narrow
 syntactic signature are detected more reliably than classes
 that depend on how a value is later used, which is the expected
 ordering for pattern-based analysis and sets the boundary for
 what this method can claim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Detection caveat
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Findings are produced by pattern-based static analysis of server
 metadata and source code. They are risk indicators, not confirmed
 vulnerabilities: the scanner cannot establish reachability or
 exploitability. Precision and recall against a manually labelled
 sample are reported in RQ4, and results are presented in
 aggregate without naming individual servers.
